# OpenTan privacy policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

OpenTan does not collect any data.

## What the app stores

OpenTan saves one file on your device: the game in progress, so you can close
the app and carry on later. It holds the board, the players' names as you typed
them, and the state of the game. It never leaves your device, and deleting the
app deletes it.

## What the app does not do

- It has no accounts and no sign in.
- It makes no network requests. The app has no online or multiplayer features.
- It contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third party
  software development kits.
- It does not read your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to anybody else.

## Children

OpenTan collects nothing from anybody, including children.

## Source code

OpenTan is open source under the MIT licence. You can read every line of it at
https://github.com/Sausagerolls/OpenTan and check these claims for yourself.
